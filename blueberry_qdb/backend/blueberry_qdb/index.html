OAuth and App Check renewal

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored and rotated in the Home Assistant integration. Its diagnostic sensors show expiry and sync times. This bridge stores App Check separately and automatically opens MyQ and waits up to 60 seconds for a newer token near expiry.

Android environment

Optional email login recovery
Configure MyQ email/password and a TLS IMAP mailbox before enabling recovery. Only new verification messages from MyQ for that login attempt are used. Recovery takes at most three minutes and stops on an unrecognized screen. The phone must be unlocked. No logout is required.
